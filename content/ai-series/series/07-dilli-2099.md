# Series 07: "Dilli 2099" (दिल्ली 2099)

Sci-fi dystopian romance · UA13 · 5 episodes x 12 shots · slug `dilli-2099`
Shared setup, delivery format, ffmpeg and checklist are in `00_START_HERE.md`. This file is only the content.

## 1. Series bible

| | |
|---|---|
| Title | Dilli 2099 (दिल्ली 2099), "Delhi 2099" |
| Language | Hindi (Hinglish-tinged, everyday spoken) |
| Genre | Sci-fi dystopian romance |
| Look | Retro-futurist neon-and-dust cel-shaded anime-style illustration, **not photoreal** (see section 2) |
| Rating | UA13: dystopian peril, a child in danger, no gore, no strong language |
| Setting | Delhi in 2099, a fictional walled city: white air-purifier spires and the rich Upper City inside a sealed dome; the poor Lower City lives under sagging Yamuna flyovers in orange smog. All tech and the "Vayukosh Corporation" are fictional. |
| Premise | Jai, a delivery drone pilot from the Lower City, crashes onto the rooftop of Anaya, daughter of the Vayukosh Corporation that sells filtered air. They fall for each other as Anaya finds out her father's corporation is hiding a source of naturally clean air outside the Wall. |
| Hook per episode | Ep 1: security drones hunt Jai. Ep 2: Anaya's father reaches for the wardrobe. Ep 3: the father says "you finally opened that door". Ep 4: Pari is taken hostage. Ep 5: Anaya is told she is not his daughter and was born in the clean valley (sets up season 2) |

Why this look: bold outlines, flat cel shading and a limited neon palette hide the weaknesses of current video models
(face drift, uncanny faces, skin-tone realism), keep motion simple and readable in 5 seconds, and suit a retro
sci-fi romance. It is a deliberate choice for the pilot.

## 2. Look and style (paste into every prompt)

STYLE_PREFIX:
```
retro-futurist cel-shaded anime-style illustration, bold ink outlines, flat color shading with halftone dots, vivid magenta and cyan neon against smog-orange haze, dusty film grain, 1980s sci-fi poster feel, white air-purifier spires above sagging concrete flyovers, vertical 9:16 composition
```

NEGATIVE (for models that take one):
```
photorealistic, real person, celebrity, text, watermark, logo, brand name, subtitles, extra fingers, deformed hands, distorted face, blurry, low quality, 3D render, gore, western city
```

Keep palette, grain and costume identical across all 60 shots. Change only the shot content.
